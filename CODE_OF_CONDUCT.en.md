# Code of conduct

## Commitment

The community should be safe and respectful regardless of age, experience, disability, education, nationality, race, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation or socioeconomic background. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Expected conduct

Respect different experiences and use cases. Discuss evidence, code and reproducible results. Give specific constructive feedback, accept corrections, take responsibility for mistakes and protect others' privacy, works, voices, accounts and keys.

Unacceptable conduct includes threats, stalking, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, abusive use of another person's voice, unauthorized disclosure of personal data/media, deliberately posting secrets or malicious content, and disruption that prevents collaboration.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to official issues, discussions, PRs, reviews and public representation of the project—not unrelated private expression.

Use [Support](SUPPORT.en.md) for software questions. Report harassment/privacy/safety concerns privately through **Security → Report a vulnerability**, with “Community conduct report” in the title. Supply only necessary facts, dates and links; do not attach unrelated private data.

Maintainers protect reporter privacy where reasonably possible, verify facts and may issue private corrections, warnings, temporary restrictions, content removal or permanent bans. Threats or platform violations may be escalated to GitHub or appropriate authorities. Maintainers are bound by the same rules; request review through the private channel with additional evidence.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for this project's scale and privacy boundaries.
